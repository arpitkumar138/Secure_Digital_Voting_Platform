Built With Security in Mind

Important security layers throughout the voting process.

🪪

Voter Verification

Voters must provide a registered Voter ID before accessing the voting process.

📱

OTP Authentication

A temporary OTP is sent to the registered mobile number for identity verification.

🔐

Vote Protection

The system prevents a verified voter from submitting multiple votes.

How It Works

A simple three-step voting process.

1

Verify Voter ID

Enter your registered Voter ID to start the verification process.

2

Verify OTP

Enter the temporary OTP sent to your registered mobile number.

3

Cast Your Vote

Select your choice and submit your vote through the voting interface.

About SecureVote 2.0

SecureVote 2.0 is a student-built digital voting platform prototype focused on demonstrating voter verification, OTP authentication and controlled vote submission through a web-based system.
